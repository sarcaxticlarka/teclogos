About Us
TecLogos is dedicated to providing a quality project work in a schedule-conscious, safe and cost-effective way.

TecLogos Automation Pvt Ltd is a professional engineering and integration firm that specializes in the design and implementation of high-value industrial automation solutions. TecLogos supports Automation OEM’s in domestic in all major verticals such as Power Plant, Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, Refineries, Water treatment,Cement, Steel, Paper, Metal etc.
We offer our customers a single source of vendor-independent expertise in all technologies that encompass industrial automation – from instrumentation and electrical engineering (I&E), to the design and integration of control and data gathering systems, through development of critical information and data management systems. In addition, TecLogos provides complete communication and network design services to enable the flow of data from measurement and control devices through the automation infrastructure into operations centers and corporate databases.

Principles
To be a Successful Enduring Business
To Focus on Design, Engineering and special services
To be a Good Place to Work
To Reward Effort
To be Owned and Managed by the Employees
